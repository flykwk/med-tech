## Shape and borders

- **Radius:** small [4px], medium [8px], large [16px], full [pill / circle]
- **Borders:** default [1px], emphasis [2px], color from neutral tokens

## Rules

- Higher elevation means higher priority and a temporary overlay.
- Use one radius family consistently across components.
